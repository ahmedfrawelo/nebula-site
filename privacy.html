Privacy policy

Updated September 30, 2026. This policy describes the Nebula browser extension and this information website. Contact: ahmed.kamal.frawelo@gmail.com.

Local workspace

Tabs, saved links, collections, notes, preferences and workspace records are stored in browser storage. Features that fetch link information contact the corresponding websites. Optional history search reads browser history on your device.

Local Photos stores image files you explicitly import in account-separated browser IndexedDB. These local copies are not automatically uploaded or synchronized and are not part of the encrypted password vault. Deleting a local copy does not delete its original file. Clearing browser data can remove the local copies.

Google Drive metadata

The Drive browser requests read-only file metadata for your signed-in Google account to list files and folders, search names and show file links. OAuth tokens stay in the extension background worker. This feature does not upload files, modify Drive files, or read their content. Opening a file takes you to its Google Drive or Google Docs page.

Accounts and cloud storage

Google sign-in provides your account identifier, name and email address. Email/password accounts use Supabase authentication. Account-connected workspace records and account vault data are stored in the configured Supabase service. Authentication messages, including confirmation and password recovery messages, are delivered through Brevo and contain your email address and authentication message content.

Vault protection

Account-managed vault records are encrypted, and the account service also stores the secret needed to open that vault for your authenticated account. This mode is not end-to-end encryption against the service operator. It avoids a separate master-password prompt for an account-managed vault. Older independent vaults may still require their original password to unlock and migrate. Guest vault access uses a locally stored secret. Protect your device and signed-in account.

Google and Gmail data

When you connect Gmail, Nebula uses Google APIs directly from the browser to obtain message lists, sender and recipient details, subjects, message content, labels and attachments needed by the mail interface. It sends composed messages and attachments and changes message labels only through mail features you use. Gmail access tokens are handled by the extension background worker; account metadata is stored separately.

Mailbox content is not automatically uploaded to the Nebula workspace service. If you choose Save to Nebula, the resulting workspace item includes message metadata and a short snippet and may be synchronized with your account workspace. Unsaved drafts and selected attachments remain in page memory; closing the page can lose unsaved work. If you choose Save draft to Gmail, Nebula uploads the message and attachments directly to the selected Gmail account as a draft for later editing. This does not send the message or copy it into Nebula workspace storage.

Email HTML is sanitized and displayed in a sandboxed frame. Remote images are blocked until you allow them for a message or sender. Loading an image contacts its host and can reveal your IP address and that the image was opened. Sender preferences are separated by connected account.

Optional desktop mail notifications check unread message identifiers. Notification text does not include sender, subject or message body. Browser and operating-system notification settings still apply.

Use and sharing limits

Nebula uses Google user data to provide the user-facing features described here. Nebula does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train generalized AI models. Nebula's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other optional connections

Encrypted backup features send backups to the WebDAV server or GitHub Gist you configure. Optional remote AI organization sends the relevant item titles and URLs to your chosen endpoint. Password breach checks send a short hash prefix to Have I Been Pwned, rather than the full password. These connections are separate from Gmail authorization.

Retention and your controls

Workspace records remain until deleted through the relevant features or account data is removed. Disconnecting Gmail stops Nebula's connection; it does not delete your Gmail messages or previously saved workspace items. You can revoke Nebula access in your Google Account permissions. Removing the extension removes its local browser storage, but does not itself delete cloud copies. Contact the support address above for account data access or deletion requests; identity verification may be needed. Provider backups and operational records may persist according to each provider's retention procedures.

Website and service providers

This website includes no advertising, analytics scripts or application login form. Its hosting provider processes ordinary request information, such as IP address and browser details, to deliver and protect the website. Google, Supabase, Brevo and any optional provider you configure process data needed for their respective services under their own policies.

Changes

Updates will be published here with a revised date. Material changes to how features use data will be reflected in this policy and the relevant product flow.
