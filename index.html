BROWSER WORKSPACE

Your tabs, workspace and mail.
One place to work.

Nebula is a browser extension for organizing tabs, collections, notes and saved links, with an integrated Gmail client and account-connected workspace storage.

Work with your email

Connect a Google account to read and search Gmail, compose messages with attachments, reply, organize conversations and receive optional private notifications. Gmail actions use the account permissions you approve with Google.

Choose your workspace

Organize browser content locally, or sign in for account-connected features. Account-managed vault access follows your signed-in account. See the privacy policy for how cloud storage and encryption work.

Availability

Nebula is currently under development. Google authorization is in production and is no longer limited to an approved test-user list. Google verification of Gmail access is still pending; unverified-app warnings and user limits may apply. This website does not claim Google verification or general availability.
